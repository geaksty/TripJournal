TripJournal – Privacy statement

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Who

TripJournal is a personal application of the owner of the GitHub account geaksty, used only for his own car. See the terms of use.

What data

  • From the Volvo Cars API, after consent via Volvo ID: odometer, vehicle location, engine status and trip statistics of the vehicles linked to the signed-in Volvo ID.
  • From the trip journal export of the Volvo Cars website: trips with times, addresses, odometer readings and consumption.
  • Entered by the owner: name, license plate, work address, the employer's e-mail address and notes per trip.

Why

Only to keep the owner's kilometre log (business and private trips) and to send the monthly mileage claim to his employer. Trips to or from the work location are recognised by distance to that location.

Where and how long

All data is stored only on the owner's own device in his home network. The monthly report is sent by e-mail from the owner's own mailbox to his employer. No data is stored on this website. Addresses of positions may be looked up with the public Dutch address service PDOK. Data is kept as long as needed for the owner's administration and can be deleted by the owner at any time.

Sharing

Data is not sold, not shared with other parties, and not used for advertising or profiling.

Your choices

Access to the Volvo Cars API can be withdrawn at any time by signing out in TripJournal or by revoking access in the Volvo ID account. Questions can be raised via the issues of the GitHub repository.
