TripJournal – Terms of use

Last updated: 12 November 2026

1. What TripJournal is

TripJournal is a personal, non-commercial application. Its owner (GitHub account geaksty) uses it to keep a kilometre log and monthly mileage expense claims for his own car. It runs on a single device in the owner's home network. It is not offered to the public, has no user accounts for third parties, and is not sold or licensed to anyone.

2. Use of the Volvo Cars API

With the consent given by signing in with Volvo ID, TripJournal reads data of the vehicles linked to that Volvo ID through the Volvo Cars API: odometer, vehicle location, engine status and trip statistics. It only reads data; it does not send commands to the vehicle. Consent can be withdrawn at any time by signing out in TripJournal or by revoking access in the Volvo ID account.

3. Data

Vehicle data is processed and stored only on the owner's own device. It is used solely to create the owner's kilometre log and mileage claims, which the owner sends to his employer. Data is not sold, shared with other parties or used for advertising or profiling. See the privacy statement for details.
